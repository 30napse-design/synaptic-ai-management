# Petty cash and delegated administration

- `/finance/petty-cash` replaces the old active Sheet shortcut. `petty_cash` permits company petty-cash history and receipt entry, not the general financial ledger, customers, research files, account management or bank data. Existing personal task access stays available.
- `institute_admin` makes a local account a full application manager on every request. It is explicit, absent from defaults, editable only by existing managers and recorded in the permissions audit. Laboratory membership still clears managerial authority and overrides broad old grants. It does not grant ownership of hosting or the owner's external connected services.
- Custodians create expenses, save drafts, attach private files up to 25 MB or HTTPS evidence and submit for financial review. Submitted/approved rows cannot be silently rewritten. Managers/financial approvers record charges, approve/return costs, void with a reason and maintain monthly opening figures.
- Imported Sheet rows retain month/date/document/category/description/charge/expense, source and previous balances, differences, cash withdrawal/balance, notes and original row. Formula-only rows are retained in the source snapshot rather than invented as expenses. No historical row becomes a verified bank transaction.
- Monthly balances use the documented month opening plus historical and submitted/approved entries. They are explicitly provisional until reviewed; drafts/returned/void rows are excluded. Missing month opening is shown as unknown. Do not assume the November source opening was bank-confirmed.
- Internal bank-paid expenses remain in the general financial workspace; cash costs use this dedicated ledger. Importing old costs does not manufacture duplicate accounting or bank transactions.
- `SYNAPSE_PROVISION_REQUEST` is an optional secret runtime configuration for a short-lived administrative request. It binds one opaque token hash, one exact request-body hash, an expiry and a one-use marker. Without explicit configuration it returns 404. The prepared request creates the two specifically authorized accounts and imports the source atomically; replay, different bodies and expired requests cannot create arbitrary accounts. Remove the runtime request after successful provisioning.
- Initial passwords must be changed by each user at first login. Passwords and the source payload are not committed in source or stored as plaintext in the database.
